# Audit ledger: Declarations of Independence: the American Revolution

Written source-first from `research/age-of-revolutions--american-revolution.md` (about 117 verified facts). Audited 2026-11-11 by two independent passes, mainly against English Wikipedia raw text plus 30 web searches:
- Pass A (top-down): 118 claims confirmed, 2 wrong, about 10 imprecise.
- Pass B (bottom-up): about 150 claims confirmed, 1 wrong (the same one as A), about 9 imprecise, 4 unverified.

## Changes made
| Claim as drafted | Verdict | Change |
|---|---|---|
| William Franklin "never spoke to his father again" | WRONG (A, B) | They met once more, briefly, in 1785 |
| "about 600 enslaved people lived at Monticello" | WRONG (A), IMPRECISE (B) | Over 600 in his lifetime, over 400 of them at Monticello |
| Free share of the Upper South's Black population "8.3 per cent" | Conflicting Wikipedia figures (8.3% vs nearly 10%) | "between about 8 and 10 per cent" |
| Black men "officially barred … from 1775 to 1778" | IMPRECISE (A, B) | Barred late 1775; free Black veterans readmitted within weeks; wider enlistment from 1777; enslaved men from 1778 |
| Virginia bounty "in 1781" | IMPRECISE (A) | "recruiting act of late 1780" (confirmed by A from the act's text) |
| Jasanoff's "about 8,000 free Black people" | UNVERIFIED (A), weakly confirmed (B) | Removed |
| "About 34,000 refugees reached Nova Scotia" | IMPRECISE (A, B) | "Some 34,000 to 36,000 … (which then included New Brunswick)" |
| New Jersey women voting "for about 25 years" | IMPRECISE (A, B) | "from 1776 (explicitly from 1790) until 1807" |
| Pennsylvania 1776 vote for "almost all men" | IMPRECISE (A, B) | "all taxpaying men" |
| Tea Party men "disguised as Native Americans" | IMPRECISE (A) | "some disguised as Mohawks" |
| Fugitive slave clause "strengthened" | IMPRECISE (A) | "a fugitive slave clause was added" (the Articles had none) |
| Republican synthesis "from the 1960s" | IMPRECISE (A) | "from the 1960s and 1970s" (Pocock 1975) |
| Brant "wrote in 1783"; "two million acres … now the Six Nations Reserve" | IMPRECISE (B) | "Brant protested…"; tract size estimates from about 550,000 acres up; a small part is today's reserve |
| Haudenosaunee named Washington "Town Destroyer" after Sullivan | IMPRECISE (B) | Name dates from the 1750s; it stuck after 1779 (text and quiz) |
| Quiz rubric expected "Hartz" (not in text) | Consistency (A) | Removed |
